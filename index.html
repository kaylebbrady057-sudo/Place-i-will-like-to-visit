POLAND



DESCRIPTION

Poland is a geographically diverse and culturally rich country in Central Europe, stretching from the sandy beaches of the Baltic Sea in the north to the rugged peaks of the Carpathian and Sudeten mountains in the south. It is a land where meticulously rebuilt medieval cities seamlessly blend with expansive primeval forests, making it one of Europe's most captivating destinations for both history enthusiasts and nature lovers


PLACES TO VISIT

  • Kraków
  • Gdańsk
  • Wrocław
  • Warsaw

Things To Do

  • Descend into the Rynek Underground (Kraków)
  • Listen to Chopin in Łazienki Park (Warsaw)
  • Explore Jewish Heritage in Kazimierz

POPULAR FOODS

  • Pierogi
  • Bigos (Hunter's Stew)
  • Gołąbki (Cabbage Rolls)
  • Kotlet Schabowy
